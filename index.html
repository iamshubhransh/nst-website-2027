Admission Process

Your gateway to NST
  1. 01
    Register for NSATPay for NSAT Exam & pick a slot
  2. 02
    Appear for NSATSubjects include Mathematics, English and General Aptitude
    Apply Now Prep kit
    3 mock tests will be unlocked after registration for best preparation
  3. 03
    Online Interview RoundYou will be assessed on problem solving skills & given the opportunity to showcase your projects & achievements
  4. 04
    CounsellingOnce you clear the interview, you’ll be invited to the University campus counselling session
  5. 05
    Block your seatReceive your admission letter upon successful completion of the above steps
  6. 06
    Pack your bags for Campus & HostelFinally, head to the campus & settle in. Welcome to NST!
