Rebuilding how India learns tech.

Newton School has taught coding since 2019. In 2023 it opened Newton School of Technology, a four-year B.Tech in CS & AI.

The founders’ noteSiddharth Maheshwari, co-founder1:59

The founders.

IIT Roorkee batchmates, building since 2019.

Distinguished Young Alumni, IIT RoorkeeBoth co-founders, 2022. Reported by APN News.

How it started.

From a Q&A platform to four campuses.

  1. 2019

    Two IIT Roorkee batchmates start Bolo.

    A video-based Q&A platform: the seed of Newton School.

  2. 2019

    Newton School begins.

    Coding, taught online. By 2021 its learners were working at 800+ companies.

    4,500+4,500+learners placed800+800+companies

  3. 2023

    Newton School of Technology opens.

    The first batch starts a four-year B.Tech in CS & AI, on campus.

  4. 2024

    A first-year lands Google Summer of Code.

    A ₹1.25 lakh internship, in her first year of the B.Tech.

  5. 2025

    The first batch goes to work.

    By their second year, most hold a paid internship.

    93%93%of the 2023 batch

    Meet 28 of themStudent directory
  6. 2026

    Four campuses.Now

    Delhi NCR, Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

  7. 2027

    The first batch graduates.Next
