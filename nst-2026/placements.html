FAQs

Placement questions, answered.

NST offers strong placement support with placements in top tech companies, industry partnerships, and internship opportunities.

The first batch of NST undergraduate programs is yet to graduate. Newton School has placed 4,500+ students through its online Full Stack Development & Data Science programs, with a 98% placement rate, 800+ hiring partners, and mentorship from 1,000+ industry experts, including MAANG professionals. This strong industry network and mentorship ecosystem will support NST students in securing internships and placements, providing guidance, interview preparation, and access to top hiring opportunities.

Most students start paid internships in their second year. In the fourth year, every student completes at least one internship, research project or industry project as part of the program.
