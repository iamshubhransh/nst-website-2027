Yes. The degrees offered at the Newton School of Technology in partnership with Rishihood University, ADY Patil University, and S-VYASA University, all are UGC-approved. The program is conducted in collaboration with these UGC-recognized and NAAC-accredited universities. This ensures that students receive a fully recognized undergraduate degree along with Newton School of Technology’s industry-aligned curriculum and hands-on tech training.

Yes, the program is fully residential, providing students with on-campus accommodation and a supportive learning environment.

The degree programme lasts four years. It follows Newton School of Technology’s unique phased learning model, covering core computer science foundations, specialization tracks, and real-world industry exposure.

Once you have completed the seat blocking formalities, you will receive an official invitation to visit the campus. Our admissions team will coordinate the visit schedule and provide all necessary details for your campus tour.

See the campuses

Living on campus at Newton School of Technology is mandatory to ensure that every student experiences a holistic and collaborative learning environment. The residential setup is designed to go beyond classroom education by fostering a strong sense of community, teamwork, and personal growth.

By staying on campus, students benefit from enhanced peer learning. They can easily collaborate on projects, exchange ideas, and support each other’s academic journeys. The close-knit residential community also helps students build lasting friendships and networks that are valuable both personally and professionally.

Additionally, being on campus provides convenient access to learning resources, labs, and extracurricular opportunities, allowing students to fully engage in all aspects of campus life. This immersive experience helps them develop essential life skills, leadership qualities, and a mindset ready for real-world challenges.

Newton School of Technology (NST) is a brand owned and operated by Incanus Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a private limited company. It is not a UGC-regulated Higher Education Institution (HEIs). NST provides industry-skilling and support services to the universities, while all degree awards are managed by the respective University.

Admissions are conducted through the NSAT (Newton Scholastic Aptitude Test) followed by an interview and counselling process.

See the admission process

Students receive a UGC-approved undergraduate degree in collaboration with the respective University they have applied for, along with Newton School of Technology’s advanced tech training certification.

Yes, the curriculum is the same across all NST campuses. However, the S-VYASA campus offers additional specialization options in Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and Tech Entrepreneurship. Apart from these, all other courses and learning experiences remain identical.

Yes, international students are welcome to apply for admission at NST. For any assistance or guidance regarding the process, please reach out to our support team at support.nst@newtonschool.co.

Yes, NST offers scholarships to support students

  • Merit-Based Scholarship: Up to 100% scholarship on first-year tuition fees, based on NSAT entrance and personal interview scores.
  • Young Women Leader Scholarship: Up to 10% scholarship on tuition fees across 4 years for female candidates with strong coding projects, extracurricular/sports achievements, or demonstrated leadership.
  • Extraordinary Achievement Scholarship: Up to 10% scholarship on tuition fees across 4 years, for students with notable extracurriculars, sports achievements, national/international Olympiad wins, competitive exam success, or strong coding projects.

This ensures deserving students receive financial assistance to pursue their tech education at NST.

Scholarship opportunities are discussed during your counselling session. Based on your NSAT and personal interview results, you will be informed about your eligibility and the applicable scholarship percentage, as awards are determined by multiple factors including academic performance, achievements, and leadership potential.

You’re eligible if you passed Class 12 with PCM or PCMB and scored more than 50% in both PCM and overall boards (2025, 2026 and 2027 pass-out).

Upon qualifying the NSAT and interview, the option to view and schedule your counselling session will be made available through the applicant portal.

The counselling session provides students with an overview of the NST campus and details about the programs, opportunities, and resources NST offers. It also includes discussion about any scholarships awarded based on the NSAT and interview performance.

Students are expected to:

  • Attend the session with one or both parents.
  • Keep the following documents ready: 12th marksheet, JEE marksheet (if applicable), and a valid identity proof.
  • Be present for any discussion regarding fees, which requires parents’ participation.

Please email our support team at support.nst@newtonschool.co stating the reason for rescheduling. Your request will be reviewed, and upon approval, you can rebook your slot through the applicant portal.
