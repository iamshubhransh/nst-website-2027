NSAT, in one look.

What it asks, and how to prepare.

Prep kit · Basic mathsTap an answer

Cinderella bought 63 shares at $12.49 each and sold them at $100.07 each seven years later, paying a 1.3% charge on both the purchase and the sale. Which best describes her overall return?

Prep kit · Advanced mathsTap an answer

A bag holds 5 red, 4 blue and 3 green balls. Two are drawn at random. What is the probability that they are different colours?

Prep kit · EnglishTap an answer

Put the four sentences in the order that makes a paragraph.

  1. It was one of the world’s first multinationals, with a monopoly on trade between the Netherlands and Asia.
  2. However, corruption, rival colonial powers and economic decline led to its dissolution in 1799.
  3. The Dutch East India Company, the VOC, was a powerful trading company founded by the Dutch Republic in 1602.
  4. It traded spices, built ships and colonised, with real military and administrative power.

Prep kit · AptitudeTap an answer

Six friends each love one food and hate another. Three love pizza. Vaibhavi and Nishant each love or hate biryani. Only one hates pasta. Vishwa loves one of pizza and burger and hates the other. Nishant loves paratha; Rutvik hates pizza. Aniket and Vaibhavi hate paratha. What does Vishwa love?

Six steps. Start to seat.

From registration to your first day.

  1. Step 1

    Register

    Pick a test slot. Our team calls you to help with the rest.

  2. Step 2

    Take NSAT

    Online, from home: basic and advanced maths, English and general aptitude.

  3. Step 3

    Online interview

    Talk through how you solve problems, and show your projects and achievements.

    Rishihood adds a group discussion.
  4. Step 4

    Counselling on campus

    Clear the interview, then book a counselling slot at the campus.

  5. Step 5

    Block your seat

    Receive your admission letter, then submit your documents and fees.

  6. Step 6

    Move in

    Head to the campus and settle in.

FAQs

Admissions questions, answered.

Through profile-based shortlisting, applicants with proven academic strength and analytical capability may be considered for direct interview evaluation without the NSAT. Profiles are evaluated on a JEE scorecard, academic excellence, coding profiles or competitive-programming rating, or technical achievements, and our admissions team will reach out to the shortlisted students.

You’re eligible if you passed Class 12 with PCM or PCMB and scored more than 50% in both PCM and overall boards (2025, 2026 and 2027 pass-out).

All interviews are conducted online.

Candidates applying to Rishihood University will be required to attend a Group Discussion round.

NST’s financial partners can provide loans for up to 95% of the programme fees to the admitted students.
